I study how the primate brain computes the value of choice options — and where in the visual pathway that computation begins.

Research

My dissertation asks whether the inferotemporal cortex (ITC) encodes offer value upstream of the orbitofrontal cortex (OFC) during economic choice. Using single-unit electrophysiology in macaques performing a two-alternative juice-choice task, I record from ITC and OFC to test where visual object identity is first transformed into a value signal.

In parallel, I characterize quantity and spatial tuning of anterior ITC neurons, examining how value-related signals interact with the region's canonical visual selectivity.
